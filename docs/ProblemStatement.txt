# Urban Company – Graduation Project
## Understanding and Improving Repeat Bookings Through an AI-Native Customer Experience

# SECTION 1: PROJECT OVERVIEW & PROBLEM STATEMENT

## 1.1 Business Context

Urban Company is a technology-enabled platform that connects customers with professionals for various home services, including deep cleaning, AC servicing, plumbing, electrical repairs, salon services and other household requirements.

A customer may book a service, receive the service successfully and complete the payment. However, completing one successful booking does not necessarily mean that the customer will return to Urban Company for their next service requirement.

While some customers complete another booking within 90 days, others do not return during this period.

As a Product Manager on Urban Company's Consumer Growth Team, the objective is to understand the factors influencing this behaviour and identify opportunities to improve repeat bookings through customer-centric product experiences.

## 1.2 Business Objective

Increase the percentage of customers who complete another service booking within 90 days of their initial completed booking.

### Business Metric Definition

- Select a particular month for analysis.
- Count each customer once, based on their first completed booking in a core category during the selected month.
- This booking is considered the anchor booking.
- A repeat booking is any subsequent completed booking in any category within 90 days of the anchor booking.
- InstaHelp (quick-service housekeeping) is excluded.
- Redos, warranty claims and bookings that end in a refund do not count as repeat bookings.

## 1.3 Problem Statement

Customers who complete an Urban Company service booking may not necessarily consider the platform when they have another household service requirement.

Even when their previous booking was completed successfully, customers may choose local service providers, rely on recommendations from friends or neighbours, explore alternative platforms, or postpone their household service requirements.

The underlying reasons behind these decisions are not yet clearly understood.

There is an opportunity to investigate how customers recognise household service needs, plan their requirements, evaluate service providers, make booking decisions and decide whether to return to Urban Company.

The challenge is to identify the customer behaviours, unmet needs and barriers that influence repeat booking decisions, including situations where customers have had no negative experience with their previous booking.

## 1.4 Key Problem to Investigate

**Why do customers who successfully complete an Urban Company booking not necessarily return to the platform for another service within 90 days, and what customer needs or barriers influence their decision to return or choose an alternative?**

## 1.5 Research Objectives

The project will investigate four key areas.

### A. Customer Behaviour
- How do customers identify and recognise their household service requirements?
- Are household services generally planned in advance or requested only when an immediate need arises?
- What influences customers to book a service through Urban Company for the first time?

### B. Post-Booking Experience
- What do customers expect after completing a service?
- Do customers have additional household service requirements following their initial booking?
- How do customers decide what to do when another service requirement arises?

### C. Repeat Booking Decisions
- What motivates customers to return to Urban Company?
- Why do some customers choose local providers, other platforms or personal recommendations instead?
- What prevents customers from considering Urban Company for subsequent service requirements?
- Does a satisfactory previous experience influence their next booking decision?

### D. Customer Segmentation
- How do repeat booking behaviours differ across service categories?
- Are there differences between customers across cities and household types?
- Do customers who book frequently behave differently from customers who book occasionally?

## 1.6 Expected Product Opportunity

Identify a customer-centric product opportunity that addresses a validated customer need and makes Urban Company more relevant when customers have subsequent household service requirements.

The proposed experience should provide meaningful value to customers while supporting Urban Company's repeat-booking objective.

The final product opportunity and solution will be determined through AI-powered discovery, metric decomposition, customer interviews and MVP validation.

## 1.7 Product Constraints

The proposed solution must:

- Work from the customer's perspective.
- Not depend on discounts, coupons or memberships.
- Not involve pricing changes.
- Not change service professionals' earnings or management.
- Not automatically book services or take actions on a customer's Urban Company account.
- Not message Urban Company or service professionals.
- Not scrape Urban Company or access restricted customer information.
- Address genuine customer needs rather than encourage unnecessary service bookings.

# SECTION 2: EIGHT PROJECT REQUIREMENTS

## Requirement 1: Build an AI-Powered Discovery Engine

### Objective
Build an AI-powered system that analyses publicly available customer feedback and conversations to identify potential problems, unmet needs and opportunities influencing repeat bookings.

### Data Sources

Analyse publicly accessible information from sources such as:

- Apple App Store reviews.
- Google Play Store reviews.
- Trustpilot and consumer complaint forums.
- Reddit discussions from Indian city and household communities.
- Public social media conversations about home services.
- YouTube comments on home-service and cleaning videos.
- Urban Company's public shareholder letters.
- Public earnings-call transcripts.
- Other publicly available conversations about booking home services in India.

### What the Discovery Engine Should Identify

- Customer expectations after completing a service.
- How customers identify and plan their household service requirements.
- Why customers return or do not return.
- Customer preferences for local service providers, recommendations and alternative platforms.
- Common customer frustrations and unmet needs.
- Differences across service categories, cities and household types.
- Potential product opportunities that could influence repeat bookings.

### Expected Output

The discovery engine should:

1. Collect and analyse publicly available feedback.
2. Identify and classify customer themes.
3. Quantify recurring themes where possible.
4. Compare potential opportunity areas.
5. Generate evidence-backed insights.
6. Validate AI-generated insights against manually labelled examples.
7. Account for public-review bias and the lack of feedback from customers who simply never return.

**Important:** The engine must go beyond sentiment analysis and review summarisation.

## Requirement 3: Validate the Opportunity Through User Research

### Objective
Validate AI-generated insights through interviews with actual Urban Company customers.

### Research Requirements

- Conduct interviews with 5–6 actual Urban Company users.
- Respondents must have completed an Urban Company booking within the last six months.
- Include customers who booked again within 90 days.
- Include customers who did not book again within 90 days.
- Cover more than one service category.
- Record the total number of bookings made by each respondent.

### Interview Areas

Understand:

1. How customers decided to book their initial service.
2. How they discovered Urban Company.
3. What happened after the service was completed.
4. What customers expected from the service.
5. What additional household service requirements they had afterwards.
6. How they handled subsequent service requirements.
7. Whether they considered Urban Company again.
8. What motivated them to return or prevented them from returning.
9. Whether household services are generally planned, reactive or avoided.

### Expected Output

- Interview questionnaire.
- Anonymised respondent profiles.
- Interview findings.
- Comparison of repeat and non-repeat customers.
- Common behavioural patterns.
- Insights that validate or challenge AI discovery findings.

**Privacy restriction:** Do not collect booking screenshots, invoices, addresses, phone numbers or other personal documents.

### Objective

# SECTION 4: PROJECT EXECUTION PRINCIPLES

The project should follow an evidence-first Product Management approach.

1. Do not assume the customer problem before analysing the evidence.
2. Do not select the MVP before completing primary research.
3. Distinguish customer problems from business objectives.
4. Support product decisions with evidence from discovery and interviews.
5. Validate AI-generated insights instead of accepting them blindly.
6. Focus on meaningful customer value rather than artificially increasing bookings.
7. Ensure the final MVP is functional, deployed and testable.
